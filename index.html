Useful from the first use

Start with Free.

  • 12 active products, one editable Project Pan and one active goal.
  • Use history, cost per use, amounts remaining, a cover photo and one dated progress-photo check-in per product.
  • No-buy calendar, empties, a basic monthly card and PDF.
  • Photo-inclusive backup, archived-copy import, Trash, restore and essential corrections stay Free.

When your shelf grows

Optional Pro.

  • More products, projects and goals.
  • Additional dated photo history, advanced insights, CSV and more card styles.
  • Annual subscription or one-time Lifetime option, managed by Apple.
  • Existing records and photos remain accessible after Pro expires.

Localized prices appear in Apple’s purchase sheet. No physical product is sold in the app.

Your shelf stays yours

Local, deliberate and private.

No developer collection, ads, analytics, tracking, cloud account or shopping feed. Camera and photo choices stay local. Reminders are optional and generic. You choose where exports go.

Dates are reminders to review your own product label and condition. FinishShelf does not assess product safety or give medical advice. Cost per use reflects your records; it is not a savings claim.
