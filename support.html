FinishShelf support

A little help
for your shelf.

Native iPhone and iPad app, minimum iOS/iPadOS 15. Current status: in production, preparing the App Store review package. This guide describes the implemented workflow.

Add a product and its price

Open Shelf and tap Add. Each record represents one owned unit. Price is optional: an unknown price stays unknown, and zero is treated as an explicitly recorded zero. Choose the currency for that purchase; different currencies are not combined.

Understand cost per use

Known purchase price is divided by recorded uses plus any previous-use baseline you entered. A baseline contributes to lifetime cost per use, but not to dated monthly or Project Pan totals. No uses, unknown price and amounts below the smallest currency unit have distinct labels.

Correct a use, progress photo or outcome

Open the product’s use or progress history to correct or move the exact entry to Trash. A quick-use banner offers Undo. Empty and declutter are distinct outcomes. Reopening preserves earlier uses. Existing records and essential corrections remain accessible after Pro expires.

Track amounts and photos

Add a dated percentage or weight check-in; you may correct an estimate upward. Free includes one dated progress-photo check-in per product. You can replace its photo while correcting that same check-in. More photo history requires Pro. Trashed photos are retained for recovery and do not reset the Free limit.

Create a Project Pan

Use Projects to select products and a date range, then set the target. Completing an empty is different from logging a use. A product in multiple projects is still counted once in the monthly library total.

Mark no-buy and low-buy days

Choose a date in the Journal calendar and record what happened. An unmarked day stays unknown. A purchase on a marked no-buy day needs review and is excluded from no-buy success. The app does not infer behavior from missing entries.

Save a card, PDF or CSV

Journal → Monthly share card previews the actual snapshot before export. Names and photos are off by default. Choose Save PNG in Files, Save monthly PDF or Apple’s share sheet. A4 or Letter is selected in Settings. CSV is a Pro option in Settings. Open saved files in Files to view them; exports are independent copies.

Back up and restore the whole shelf

Settings → Save full photo backup creates a .finishshelf file containing records and retained photos, including Trash. Choose a destination in Files. Import full photo backup validates the file and shows a review before adding fresh linked archived copies. It does not replace your current products. Keep enough free space for both the backup and staged import.

Recover an unreadable library

FinishShelf keeps the unreadable original and asks you to preserve the original and photos through Files before starting an empty library or importing a backup. A preserved-original file is for recovery/support and is different from an ordinary .finishshelf backup. Do not delete the app before saving what you need.

Manage Pro and Restore Purchases

Annual Pro is an auto-renewing Apple subscription; Lifetime is a separate one-time purchase. The purchase screen displays Apple’s current localized price and duration. Use Restore Purchases with the Apple account that owns the purchase. Apple manages subscription changes and cancellations in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Existing records and photos remain accessible when Pro expires.

Permissions and reminders

Allow camera access only if you want to take a photo; the photo picker works with your selected photos. Generic local reminders are optional and can be disabled in Settings. Save Image may request add-only Photos access. Denying an optional permission does not remove your saved shelf.

Contact support

Email hello@arvinify.com with the app version, iPhone/iPad model and a short description of what happened. Avoid sending private photos, backups, credentials or purchase secrets unless specifically needed and intentionally chosen. We do not receive your shelf automatically.

Product dates are for your own review. Follow the label and actual condition; FinishShelf does not determine safety. Privacy policy.
