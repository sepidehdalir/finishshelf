Your shelf stays yours.

FinishShelf: Project Pan is developed by Sepideh Javanbakht Dalir / Arvinify. It stores the records you create locally and does not send your shelf data to the developer.

Local records and photos

Your products, prices, uses, dates, goals, no-buy marks, notes, photos and settings stay in the app’s local storage. There is no app account, developer server, analytics SDK, advertising SDK or cross-app tracking. FinishShelf does not provide cloud synchronization. Your device or chosen file provider may independently back up data under your settings and its own policies.

Camera and photo choices

The camera is used only when you choose to take a product or progress photo. Apple’s photo picker gives the app only the photos you select. Imported photos are resized locally and stored as JPEG copies, with private location and camera metadata removed. Technical image dimensions and color information may remain. The app does not upload these photos to the developer.

When you deliberately choose Save Image in Apple’s share sheet, the system may ask for permission to add the card to Photos. This does not give FinishShelf general access to read your photo library.

Optional reminders

Local notifications require your permission. Reminder text is generic and does not contain product names, prices or photos. You can turn reminders off in FinishShelf or iOS Settings. Review dates are reminders, not product-safety assessments.

Purchases

Apple processes Annual and Lifetime Pro purchases and restores. FinishShelf reads verified purchase and entitlement information through StoreKit to enable features. The developer does not receive your payment-card information, billing credentials or Apple password through the app.

Files, exports and sharing

You choose whether and where to save a photo-inclusive backup, card, PDF or CSV, or share an image through Apple’s share sheet. A full backup contains your records and retained photos, including Trash. Monthly card product names and photos are off by default; private notes are excluded from cards, PDFs and CSV. Files you save to another app or cloud provider are handled by that destination.

Retention and control

Trash is recoverable. A backup import creates fresh linked archived copies, without silently replacing the current library. To remove the app’s local library, delete the app. Save a photo-inclusive backup first if you want to retain your work. Exported copies and operating-system backups are controlled separately by you and their storage providers.

Support

If you contact hello@arvinify.com, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. Please omit passwords, payment credentials and private photos unless they are necessary and you deliberately choose to share them. The app does not automatically send a support report.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if practices change. Questions: hello@arvinify.com.
